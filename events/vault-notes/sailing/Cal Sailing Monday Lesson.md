Monday afternoon Beginning Sailing Lesson at Cal Sailing Club, Berkeley Marina.
Runs an hour later (until 5 PM) while Daylight Saving Time is in effect.

The times here are the *nominal* lesson window. The club's real open and close
times move with the tides, so `events/filters/tides` intersects this window with
the club's live schedule before it reaches the dashboard — and drops the event
entirely on a day the tide keeps the club shut past 4 PM.
