Saturday morning Beginning Sailing Lesson at Cal Sailing Club, Berkeley Marina.
The morning window does not shift with Daylight Saving Time, so there is no
`start_dst`/`end_dst` here.

Saturday is the day the tide clamp bites most often: a low tide that holds the
club closed until the early afternoon wipes out the whole 10 AM-1 PM window, and
`events/filters/tides` drops the event rather than showing hours you cannot sail.
